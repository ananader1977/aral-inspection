Job life cycle

From request to inspector commission

Every job moves through these statuses. Each change is logged with who, when and a note, and the app blocks skipped steps. Bank clients on credit skip payment and are invoiced monthly.

New request→ Quoted→ Quote accepted→ Agreement signed→ Paid (demo card)→ Scheduled→ Inspected→ Report draft→ Under review→ Report issued→ Invoiced→ Closed

Design choices worth checking

  • Property is separate from job, so a re-inspection or a later resale of the same unit reuses it and shows its history.
  • All fixed wording is versioned (content_blocks): changing the T&Cs never alters a report already issued; each PDF records the version it used.
  • Photos can exist without an observation (quick mode) and are assigned later. Each photo carries a phone-generated ID, so a retry after weak signal never creates duplicates.
  • VAT is a switch: until you register, invoices show no VAT; afterwards new ones become Tax Invoices at 5%.

Your call

Six decisions before Phase 2

  1. Severity scale. Red Critical and blue Attention as in the sample, plus a third Informational for items tested OK?
    Recommended: yes
  2. Indicative repair cost per finding: print on the PDF, or keep internal?
    Recommended: internal for resale, optional for snagging
  3. Videos. A PDF can't play video, so each video prints as a thumbnail with a QR link to the online report. OK?
    Recommended: yes
  4. Report summary. List red and blue findings (as the sample does), or red only?
    Recommended: red and blue, ticked per finding
  5. Agreement step. Client e-signs the service agreement after accepting the quote and before paying (as in the GTA flow)?
    Recommended: yes, one click with typed name
  6. Terms & conditions text. I will load placeholder T&Cs marked "draft". The final wording should come from a UAE lawyer, not the sample.
    Action: send me approved text when ready
