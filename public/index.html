Questions

What do you need from me on the day?

Access to the property, with the air conditioning and DEWA connected. Without them we cannot test cooling, electrics or plumbing properly.

How long does an inspection take?

About 2 to 3 hours for an apartment and 4 to 6 hours for a villa.

Do you do valuations?

No. We inspect condition. For a valuation we work with a RERA-registered valuation partner and can pass on our inspection to them.

Will you fix the defects?

No. We never sell repairs, so our report stays independent. We tell you which trade to call for each finding.
